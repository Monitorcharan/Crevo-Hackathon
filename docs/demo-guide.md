# Crevo hackathon demo

[Live app](https://crevo-hackathon.onrender.com) · [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Monitorcharan/Crevo-Hackathon)

## Before recording

- Open the live app once and let the free Render service wake up.
- Open `/login` and use the one-click shared creator and brand demo accounts, ideally in separate browser profiles. The creator already has an illustrative portfolio item; the brand has a sample brief. Shared-account edits persist and are visible to other visitors.
- Prepare a short campaign idea, such as: “Launch a new café with a 15-second vertical film showing the space, signature drinks, and people behind it.”

## Submission summary

Crevo is a live creator discovery and collaboration platform built with React, FastAPI, Supabase, and Gemini. Creators publish profiles and AI work portfolios. Brands draft structured briefs, review explainable matches, accept applications, and collaborate through project messages. The public demo includes clearly labeled fictional creator profiles; real users can register and complete the full workflow.

## Scope to state clearly

Project messages update after sending or refreshing. Payments, milestones, and shared project files are outside this MVP. Portfolio tool and ownership claims are creator reported.
