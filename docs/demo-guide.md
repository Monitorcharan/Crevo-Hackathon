# Crevo presentation guide

Use the [complete Round 1 and Round 2 workflow](rounds-one-and-two-workflow.md) for the current live presentation, account setup, exact buttons, delivery files, verification results and limits.

The live marketplace uses the curated Crevo Studio creator and Crevo Creative brand. Public demo galleries, shared demo sign-in and sample checkout have been retired. Earlier records remain in a disabled archive. `/demo` redirects to discovery.

For isolated development fixtures only, see the historical [local Round 2 walkthrough](round-two-demo.md). Those fictional accounts and video samples are not the current live marketplace.

## Presentation sequence

1. Introduce the problem and show the live marketplace.
2. Show creator specializations, actual tools, Cobalt/Citrus evidence, offered rights and pending trust review.
3. Show the brand identity, manual discovery and structured brief.
4. Explain ranked matching, evidence links and gaps.
5. Review an actual assistant shortlist, then invite the creator.
6. Switch to the creator to accept; switch back to select and propose terms.
7. Confirm scope/license and post a production update.
8. Upload the wrong square file, then the correct vertical file.
9. Submit version 1, request a revision and submit version 2 with explicit notes.
10. Approve the latest delivery and show the retained agreement and history.

Use separate browser profiles for the two accounts. The prepared Cobalt brief is at the starting point; following these steps intentionally advances it.
